## 為什麼（because）

Adding the slot->expert column to the pool-row comparator changed what 'ids differ' licenses: it no longer implies 'the mapping is at fault' (it can be a benign relayout of the same expert) and 'ids same' no longer implies 'the bytes are at fault' (the slot can hold different experts). The pre-existing closing sentence chose between 'mapping, not bytes' and 'residency delivered different content' purely on whether the ids matched, so on a row that was a genuine content difference with different ids the tool printed a CONTENT headline and then, three lines later, the opposite conclusion. Separately, the pre-existing all-sentinel guard was written at key level because that is where the original incident was; a fixture with one unread row out of two still reported the key as SAME, because an unread row's group tuple is (sentinel, sentinel) on both sides and compares equal.

## 反例（counterexample_observed）

Both found by fixtures, not by reasoning, in the same edit (2026-09-17): the 'routing' fixture with ids differing and owners differing printed `*** CONTENT` followed by `FIRST DIFF ... ids differ. The index differs: mapping, not bytes.`; and the 'unread' fixture printed `verdict rows: SAME=1` while its own cause tally said `SAME=1 UNREAD=1`. After the fix the latter is `PART-READ=1` and withholds the verdict.
