# 25 t/s is a two-axis necessity, not a single lever

> **Superseded in part** by `docs/TARGET25_NECESSARY_CONDITIONS_2026-09-22.md`: the step axis here is
> "how much host gap is removed", measured on one server-regime log. The measured round budget shows
> that for MTP-on those rows are the small ones (the marginal token is 70–102% round remainder), and
> that even a perfect drafter fails every rung above the GPU floor. The floor (149.24), the two bounds
> and the cross-checks below still stand and are used as the cross-regime check in the new document.

**Date** 2026-09-20 · carrier `Nail-Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-MTP-UD-IQ3_XXS-denseIQ4X`, MTP on k=3, pool 8 GiB,
profile `prefill250` · step read from `Backup/cgc_logs/llama_server_20260920_023021.log`
(`CGC-DECPROF`, `ntok=4`, `segs=41`, n=107) · table generated by
`python3 scripts/check/target25_grid.py` (6/6 selftest)

## 0. One line

**`t/s = mean_len / step`. 25 t/s means `step ≤ 40 × mean_len`. Two independent axes hold it: how much
of the host-serial gap can be removed, and how many tokens each verify step emits. Today's grid has no
PASS cell; the only PASS cell in the whole table is the corner where BOTH axes are at their limits.**

## 1. The two hard bounds (both are limits of the hardware/configuration, not of effort)

| axis | bound | where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| step | **≥ 149.24 ms** | `union_sum` — the per-segment GPU spans. They are chained (attention(il+1) consumes MoE(il)), so no host-side overlap can shorten a step below their sum. Measured column, same log. |
| mean_len | **≤ 4.0** | k=3 drafts ⇒ at most 4 tokens emitted per verify step. |

**`gpu_busy_sum 181.56` is NOT the step bound.** Its own definition (`ggml-backend.cpp:2049`) is
`Σ(end-start) over the segment's n_cb+1 buffers (overlap counted twice)`; the measured ratio
`181.56 / 149.24 = 1.22` is exactly that double count. An earlier draft of this line derived
"hard ceiling 18.59" from it — withdrawn (`docs/L3_ASYNC_FILL_PLAN_2026-09-20.md` §6,
`docs/L3_M1_GAP_LEDGER_2026-09-20.md` §1).

Read the columns, not the rows: **every step row that is reachable by removing host work still fails
at every mean_len except the corner.** 24.43 at `cb+submit` hidden and ml=4.0 misses 25 by 2.3%; and
that 4.0 is accept at the k=3 ceiling, which nothing has ever measured.

## 4. What must be true (necessary conditions, each falsifiable)

```
required ml at each step, and the overlap fraction it implies
   247.98 ms  needs ml 6.199  (above the k=3 cap)   overlap   0.0% of the 98.74 ms gap
   173.80 ms  needs ml 4.345  (above the k=3 cap)   overlap  75.1%
   163.71 ms  needs ml 4.093  (above the k=3 cap)   overlap  85.3%
   149.24 ms  needs ml 3.731  (within k=3)          overlap 100.0%

feasible region for 25 t/s: step in [149.24, 160.0] ms and ml in [3.731, 4.0]
  at ml = 4.0 (accept at the k=3 ceiling) the overlap must reach 89% of the gap (248 -> 160 ms)
  at the union floor the ml must reach 3.731 (93% of the cap)
```

So 25 t/s is reachable **only** if:

1. **accept reaches the k=3 ceiling (ml 4.0)** — then **89% of the gap must go**; or
2. **accept reaches ml ≥ 3.731 (93% of the cap)** and **100% of the gap goes** — i.e. the step must hit
   the union floor exactly, which is the physical limit, not a budget.

Neither has been measured. Nothing in between qualifies: at 85.3% of the gap removed (all of `cb` and
`submit`) the requirement is still ml 4.093 > 4.0.

## 5. Which lever owns which axis

| axis | owner | today | what "done" looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| step | the dispatcher (`ggml-backend.cpp`, segment-split submit) | in-cache deliverable ≈ 8.5% (submit + commit→launch) → ~14.8 t/s | an arm where `gap` drops and `total` drops with it, `union` unchanged |
| mean_len | M4 / line S (draft-from-target-distribution, accept) | 3.375 quoted; the k=3 cap is 4.0 | a paired arm where `mean_len` and `step` are reported **together** (`scripts/check/mtp_ruler.py` exists for this) |

## 6. What this document does NOT claim

- **It does not say the gap is removable.** The gap's composition is unmeasured: it contains ~0.27
  ms/segment of commit→launch (pipeline, not removable) and an unknown split between *waiting for the
  GPU's argsort output* (the union's ids are computed on the GPU, so this is dependency latency) and
  *host work that could be issued earlier*. The next measurement is that split, not another overlap
  experiment.
- **It does not say ml 3.375 is today's mean_len in the same arm as the 247.98 step.** That pairing is
  cross-arm, the same class of error this repo keeps catching. The column values are readings in
  circulation from different arms (2.71 / 2.97 / 3.375) plus the configuration cap (4.0); each needs
  its own same-arm `mean_len` + `step` pair before it can be quoted as a rate.
- **It does not price the byte side.** A smaller model or a bigger pool moves `cb` directly, i.e. it
  buys rows of the step axis without the dispatcher — that is a quantization decision, and this table
  says nothing about it.
- **`union_sum` is measured under today's pipeline.** If the dispatcher changes segment boundaries,
  the spans change and 149.24 must be re-read on the new binary rather than carried forward.
