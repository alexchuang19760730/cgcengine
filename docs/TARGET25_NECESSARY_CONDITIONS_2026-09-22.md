## 0. One line

`t/s = mean_len / step` is still the whole target. What changed is **which rows the step axis has**:
the 09-20 table's rows were "remove host overlap from the per-layer terms", and today's round budget
shows that for MTP-on those rows are the *small* ones. **70–102% of the marginal verify token is round
remainder** — per-round work after the last layer — and removing every per-layer host term plus the
draft head is still not enough: at k=3 even a **perfect** drafter fails every rung except the GPU floor.

## 4. Which cell needs which lever

- **Every rung above the floor: needs the remainder — and no accept rate can substitute.** Three of
  four rungs need `mean_len` 5.4–9.6 against a hard cap of 4.0. So the remainder is not competing with
  accept for credit; it is the **precondition** for the accept axis to have anything to buy. That is the
  single most important correction to the 09-20 table.
- **The floor row: needs accept at α = 0.94–1.00, and the floor itself is not removable.** This is the
  same "only the corner passes" conclusion as 09-20, now with the ownership attached: the corner is
  `[remainder gone] AND [cheap drafter] AND [per-layer host gone] AND α ≥ ~0.95`.
- **Rep 2's floor (164.5) does not clear 25 t/s at any accept rate.** That is drift between reps, not a
  property of the machine — but it is the honest reason the floor row cannot be quoted as "achievable".
- **Wider k does not open a cell.** With the affine cost law (`cost = 41.74 + 51.56·T`,
  `docs/DRAFT_BUDGET_K_2026-09-22.md`), `T=8` costs 454 ms and needs `mean_len` 11.4 against a cap of 8.

## 5. What the accept axis is actually worth today

Not a route to 25, and worth saying with a number: the per-draft accept the same harness implies is
**α ≈ 0.654**, where the best k is **1**, giving **11.42 t/s = 1.066×** the MTP-off baseline
(`DRAFT_BUDGET_K` §4). The accept lever's own ceiling at k=3 is `mean_len` 4.0 ⇒ 16.13 t/s at today's
step and ~25 only at 160 ms. Alternatively, **MTP-off** needs `step ≤ 40 ms` (E=1) against today's
`S0 = 93.3 ms` ⇒ a 2.33× step cut, which no row of this ladder provides either.

## 6. Boundaries

- **Absolute steps are this window's** (swap 4.5–6.8 GB, `usable` 39.9–63.9%, thermal HEAVY). The
  **ratios** (which term owns the marginal, and the ladder's shape) are drift-robust; the t/s values are
  not comparable with the clean 12.57 family.
- The floor is `Σ wait_3` over 40 layers — the eval-sync chain, i.e. the GPU floor *as the CPU sees it*.
  Its agreement with `union_sum 149.24` is a cross-check, not an identity: they are different
  instruments on different regimes.
- `needs alpha` assumes the geometric model for `E` and k=3. It is a bound on the accept axis, not a
  measurement of any drafter.
- The remainder is measured **by subtraction**, so its size is only as good as the per-layer means in
  the same arm; its *internal* split (lm_head / sampler-argmax / accept path / inter-graph bookkeeping)
  is still unmeasured. That is the next instrument, and §4's claim that accept cannot substitute for it
  does not depend on which sub-part it is.
- **No M1/M2/M3 gate**: env-only runs, engine digest recorded per arm.
