Describe in detail the molecular mechanism of eukaryotic mRNA splicing by the spliceosome. Begin with the cis-acting sequence elements: the 5' splice site, the branch point adenosine, the polypyrimidine tract, and the 3' splice site, and explain how U1 snRNP and U2 snRNP recognize them through both RNA-RNA base pairing and protein contacts. Then walk through the two transesterification reactions, noting the role of the branch point 2'-hydroxyl as the nucleophile in the first step and the 3'-hydroxyl of the upstream exon in the second, and describe the resulting lariat intermediate and the debranching pathway. Discuss how ATP-dependent RNA helicases such as Prp2, Prp16, Prp22 and Prp43 drive the ordered rearrangements that enforce fidelity, and explain the kinetic proofreading model. Finally, contrast this with group II self-splicing introns as evolutionary evidence, and summarize how alternative splicing is regulated by SR proteins and hnRNPs in a tissue-specific manner.
